## 1. Purpose

This procedure ensures all user complaints are handled consistently, fairly, and within defined timeframes, in compliance with applicable consumer protection legislation.

---

## 2. Definition of a Complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction from a user about:
- A Metups product, service, or feature
- The conduct of Metups staff or moderation decisions
- A support interaction
- A policy decision that affects the user

*Note: Disputes between buyers and sellers about transactions are not Metups complaints but may require Metups' Trust & Safety involvement.*

## 4. Complaint Handling Process

### Step 1: Acknowledgement (within 24 hours)
- [ ] Log complaint in support system with unique reference number
- [ ] Send acknowledgement email to user with reference number and expected resolution timeline
- [ ] Assign to appropriate team member based on complaint type

### Step 2: Investigation (within 5 business days)
- [ ] Review all relevant account activity, messages, and system logs
- [ ] Gather context from all parties where applicable
- [ ] Review applicable policies
- [ ] Escalate to manager if policy clarification is needed

### Step 3: Resolution (within 5–10 business days)
- [ ] Draft resolution with clear explanation of outcome
- [ ] Have resolution reviewed by team lead for consistency
- [ ] Communicate decision in writing, explaining:
  - What was found
  - What decision was made and why
  - What action (if any) was taken
  - Right to escalate if unsatisfied

### Step 4: Escalation (if user remains dissatisfied)
- [ ] Escalate to Customer Support Manager within 2 business days of escalation request
- [ ] Manager review and final decision within 5 business days
- [ ] Communicate final decision in writing
- [ ] Advise of external escalation options (regulator, ombudsman)

### Step 5: Closure and Learning
- [ ] Mark complaint as resolved in support system
- [ ] Log root cause and resolution type
- [ ] Identify process improvement opportunities
- [ ] Report trends in monthly review

## 5. Complaint Categories and Routing

| Category | Assigned To | Escalation Path |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| Listing moderation | Trust & Safety | T&S Manager |
| Account suspension | Trust & Safety | T&S Manager → CX Manager |
| Data privacy request | DPO / Privacy | Legal |
| Technical issue | Engineering (via CX ticket) | CTO |
| Billing dispute | Billing / CX | Finance Manager |
| Staff conduct | CX Manager | CEO |
| Legal/formal complaint | Legal team | Legal Counsel |

## 7. Record Keeping

All complaints are logged with:
- Date received
- Category and nature
- Actions taken
- Outcome
- Resolution date

Records retained for **3 years** for regulatory compliance.

---

## 8. External Escalation Options

If a user is unsatisfied after exhausting our internal process:
- **Zimbabwe:** Consumer Council of Zimbabwe or relevant authority
- **South Africa:** Consumer Protection Act — National Consumer Commission
- **EU/UK users:** GDPR Supervisory Authority
