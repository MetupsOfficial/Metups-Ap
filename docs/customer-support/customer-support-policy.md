## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standards, channels, response times, and escalation paths for Metups customer support. It ensures consistent, high-quality support experiences for all users.

---

## 2. Support Channels

| Channel | Use Case | Availability |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Email: support@metups.com | General enquiries, account issues | Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 CAT |
| Email: trust@metups.com | Safety, fraud, AUP violations | Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 CAT |
| Email: privacy@metups.com | Data privacy requests | Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 CAT |
| In-app: Report button | Listing and user reports | 24/7 (reviewed during business hours) |
| Help Center: help.metups.com | Self-service articles | 24/7 |
| [Social media — if applicable] | Community queries | [Hours] |

*No phone support is currently offered.*

---

## 3. Response Time SLAs

| Priority | Criteria | First Response | Resolution |
|---------|---------|---------------|-----------|
| P1 — Critical | Account hacked, illegal content, active fraud | 2 hours | 4 hours |
| P2 — High | Listing or account suspended, trust violations | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| P3 — Medium | Account issues, billing, listing problems | 8 hours | 48 hours |
| P4 — Low | General enquiries, feature requests, feedback | 24 hours | 5 business days |

*Response times are measured during business hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 CAT).*

---

## 4. Ticket Prioritisation

Assign priority based on the following:

**P1 triggers:**
- Evidence of account compromise
- Child safety concerns
- Illegal item reported
- Extortion or credible physical threat

**P2 triggers:**
- User locked out of account
- Listing wrongly removed
- Active scam in progress

**P3 triggers:**
- Password reset failure
- Listing not displaying correctly
- Billing enquiry (future)

**P4 triggers:**
- General feedback
- Feature requests
- "How do I..." questions (redirect to Help Center first)

---

## 5. Tone and Communication Standards

- **Professional but warm:** approachable, not robotic
- **Clear and concise:** avoid jargon, use plain language
- **Empathetic:** acknowledge frustration before offering solutions
- **Accurate:** never promise what cannot be delivered
- **Timely:** always acknowledge receipt within SLA, even if resolution takes longer

---

## 6. Escalation Matrix

| Scenario | Escalation Path |
|---------|----------------|
| Legal threat or litigation | → Legal team within 1 hour |
| Media enquiry | → CEO / Communications immediately |
| Data breach report | → Security team + DPO immediately |
| Law enforcement request | → Legal team within 2 hours |
| Repeat critical offender | → Trust & Safety team |
| User threatening harm | → Trust & Safety + contact authorities |

---

## 7. Out-of-Scope Support

Metups does not provide support for:
- Disputes between buyers and sellers over transaction prices
- Recovery of money paid outside the platform
- Technical issues with third-party devices or internet connections
- Legal advice
