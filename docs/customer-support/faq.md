## General

**Q: What is Metups?**  
A: Metups is Zimbabwe's free online marketplace for buying and selling pre-owned (second-hand) goods. You can buy and sell almost anything — furniture, electronics, clothing, vehicles, and more — by meeting up locally with the other party.

**Q: Is Metups free to use?**  
A: Yes. Listing items and contacting sellers is completely free. There are no commissions or transaction fees.

**Q: Who can use Metups?**  
A: Anyone aged 18 or older with a valid email address. We currently focus on Zimbabwe but welcome users from neighbouring countries.

**Q: Is Metups available as a mobile app?**  
A: Metups is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it on your phone directly from your browser — no app store needed. On Chrome or Safari, tap the browser menu and select "Add to Home Screen."

## Accounts

**Q: How do I create an account?**  
A: Visit metups.com and tap "Sign Up." Enter your email and a secure password, or sign in instantly with your Google account.

**Q: I didn't receive my verification email. What should I do?**  
A: Check your spam/junk folder. If it's not there, wait 5 minutes and request a new one from the login page. Still not working? Email support@metups.com.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: On the login page, tap "Forgot Password," enter your email, and follow the link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can I use Metups without an account?**  
A: You can browse listings without an account, but you need an account to contact sellers, save items, or post listings.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

## Selling

**Q: How do I post a listing?**  
A: Tap the "+" (Sell) button, add photos, fill in the title, description, price, category, and location, then tap "Publish."

**Q: How many photos can I add to a listing?**  
A: Up to [X] photos per listing. We recommend at least 3 from different angles.

**Q: How do I edit or remove a listing?**  
A: Go to My Listings, tap the listing, and select "Edit" or "Delete."

**Q: How do I mark an item as sold?**  
A: Open the listing and tap "Mark as Sold." This removes it from search but keeps a record for your history.

**Q: What items are not allowed?**  
A: See our [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md) for the full list. In brief: no illegal items, no weapons, no counterfeit goods, no adult content.

**Q: Can I sell new items?**  
A: Metups is designed for pre-owned goods. Small quantities of new items may be acceptable, but commercial volume selling is not permitted.

## Buying

**Q: How do I contact a seller?**  
A: Open the listing and tap "Message Seller." This opens an in-app chat.

**Q: Is it safe to meet a stranger?**  
A: Follow our safety tips: meet in public places (shopping centres, police stations), bring a friend, inspect the item before paying, and never send money in advance.

**Q: The item is not as described. What can I do?**  
A: Unfortunately, Metups does not process payments and cannot mediate financial disputes. If you feel you've been defrauded, report the seller via trust@metups.com and consider reporting to the police.

**Q: How does the rating system work?**  
A: After a transaction, both parties can leave a star rating (1–5) and brief comment. Ratings are visible on profiles to help other users make informed decisions.

## Safety & Trust

**Q: How do I report a scam or suspicious listing?**  
A: Tap "Report" on the listing or message, or email trust@metups.com. We review reports within 24–48 hours.

**Q: What should I do if I was scammed?**  
A: Report the user to trust@metups.com and to your local police. We will cooperate with law enforcement investigations.

**Q: Does Metups verify sellers?**  
A: Currently, we verify email addresses. Identity verification for premium sellers is on our roadmap.

## Technical

**Q: The app won't load. What should I do?**  
A: Check your internet connection. Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, email support@metups.com.

**Q: Does Metups work offline?**  
A: Partially. Previously viewed listings are available offline. You cannot post listings or send messages without an internet connection.

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**  
A: Chrome (Android/Desktop), Safari (iOS/Mac), Firefox, and Edge. We recommend Chrome for the best PWA experience.

---

## Privacy & Data

**Q: Who can see my phone number?**  
A: No one. Your phone number is private and never shown to other users.

**Q: Can I download my data?**  
A: Yes. Email privacy@metups.com with subject "Data Export Request." We'll send your data within 30 days.

**Q: How do I opt out of marketing emails?**  
A: Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email, or go to Settings → Notifications → Marketing.
