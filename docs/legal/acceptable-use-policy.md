## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines standards of behaviour for all Metups users. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or legal action.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

You may use Metups to:
- Post honest listings of pre-owned items you own or have authority to sell
- Browse, save, and enquire about listings
- Communicate with other users for legitimate transaction purposes
- Rate and review transaction counterparties honestly

---

## 3. Prohibited Content

### 3.1 Illegal Items
- Drugs, controlled substances, or paraphernalia
- Firearms, weapons, or ammunition without appropriate licence
- Counterfeit or stolen goods
- Items that infringe copyright, trademark, or patent
- Wildlife, protected species, or their products

### 3.2 Dangerous Items
- Unlicensed explosives, fireworks, or flammable materials
- Recalled or safety-hazard consumer products
- Unregistered pesticides or hazardous chemicals

### 3.3 Misleading or Fraudulent Listings
- Items misrepresented in photos, description, or condition
- Fake or inflated prices to manipulate reviews
- Listings for items the seller does not possess
- Advance-fee or escrow scams

### 3.4 Inappropriate Content
- Sexually explicit or adult content
- Content targeting or endangering children
- Hateful, discriminatory, or threatening content
- Personal information of third parties without consent

---

## 4. Prohibited Behaviours

### 4.1 Platform Abuse
- Creating multiple accounts to evade suspension
- Automated bulk listing creation without approval
- Scraping, crawling, or harvesting user data
- Attempting to access accounts or systems unauthorised

### 4.2 User Harm
- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of other users
- Publishing private information about other users ("doxxing")
- Impersonating other users, Metups staff, or officials
- Manipulating ratings through fake transactions

### 4.3 Commercial Abuse
- Posting commercial (new goods) listings disguised as private sales
- Advertising services unrelated to selling goods (escort services, MLM, etc.)
- Spamming users with unsolicited messages

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

Report violations via:
- **In-app:** Listing → Report → Select reason
- **Email:** trust@metups.com
- **Emergency (illegal content):** illegal@metups.com

We aim to review reports within **24–48 hours**.

---

## 6. Enforcement

| Violation Severity | Action |
|-------------------|--------|
| Minor first offence | Warning + content removal |
| Repeated minor violations | 7-day suspension |
| Serious violation | Permanent ban |
| Illegal activity | Permanent ban + report to authorities |

---

## 7. Appeals

Appeal a decision within **14 days** by emailing appeals@metups.com with your account email and reason for appeal. Decisions are final after 30 days.
