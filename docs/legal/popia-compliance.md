## 1. Applicability

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") applies to Metups when processing personal information of South African data subjects. This statement documents Metups' compliance with POPIA's eight conditions for lawful processing.

## 3. The Eight Conditions for Lawful Processing

### Condition 1: Accountability
- [ ] Metups has appointed an Information Officer registered with the Information Regulator
- [ ] Policies and procedures are in place to ensure POPIA compliance
- [ ] Staff are trained on POPIA obligations

### Condition 2: Processing Limitation
- [ ] We collect only personal information necessary for the specific purpose
- [ ] Personal information is collected directly from data subjects where reasonably possible
- [ ] We do not retain personal information for longer than necessary

### Condition 3: Purpose Specification
- [ ] The purpose for collecting personal information is defined before collection
- [ ] Data subjects are notified of the purpose at collection
- [ ] Personal information is not retained beyond its stated purpose

**Purposes defined for Metups:**
- Marketplace account management
- Enabling transactions between buyers and sellers
- Safety and fraud prevention
- Service improvement and analytics (opt-in)
- Legal compliance

### Condition 4: Further Processing Limitation
- [ ] Personal information is not processed in a manner incompatible with the stated purpose
- [ ] Personal information is not sold to third parties for marketing

### Condition 5: Information Quality
- [ ] Processes are in place for data subjects to update their information (Settings → Profile)
- [ ] We take reasonable steps to ensure personal information is accurate and up to date

### Condition 6: Openness
- [ ] Privacy Policy is published at metups.com/privacy-policy
- [ ] Data subjects are notified of what information is collected and why
- [ ] Information Officer details are accessible

### Condition 7: Security Safeguards
- [ ] Technical security measures: HTTPS, RLS, JWT sessions, input validation
- [ ] Organisational measures: access controls, staff training, incident response
- [ ] Third-party processors are bound by POPIA-compliant agreements
- [ ] Breach notification procedure in place (72-hour target)

### Condition 8: Data Subject Participation
- [ ] Data subjects can access their personal information upon request
- [ ] Data subjects can request correction of inaccurate information
- [ ] Data subjects can object to processing
- [ ] Data subject requests are responded to within **30 days**

---

## 4. Special Personal Information

Metups does **not** intentionally process special personal information (as defined in Section 26 of POPIA) such as race, health, religion, political views, or biometric data.

---

## 5. Processing of Children's Personal Information

Metups does not allow users under 18 and takes reasonable steps to prevent processing of children's personal information (Section 35 POPIA).

---

## 6. Direct Marketing

- [ ] Marketing communications require opt-in consent
- [ ] Opt-out mechanisms are provided in all marketing emails
- [ ] A suppression list is maintained for opted-out contacts

---

## 7. Cross-Border Transfers

Personal information is only transferred to countries that provide adequate protection, or where the data subject has consented, or where appropriate contractual clauses are in place (Section 72 POPIA).
