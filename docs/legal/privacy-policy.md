## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account information:** full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, city/location, profile photo
- **Listing information:** product title, description, price, category, condition, photos, and location of item
- **Communication data:** messages exchanged with other users through our in-app chat
- **Verification data:** identity documents submitted for seller verification (where applicable)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information:** device type, operating system, browser version, screen resolution
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used, listings clicked, search queries, session duration
- **Location data:** city-level location (with your permission) to show nearby listings
- **IP address:** used for fraud prevention and approximate geolocation
- **Service Worker and PWA data:** cached assets for offline functionality

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Google OAuth:** if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google
- **Supabase Auth:** our authentication provider processes sign-in events on our behalf

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

### GDPR (EU/UK users)
- **Right to access** — request a copy of your data
- **Right to rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** — request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restriction** — limit how we process your data
- **Right to portability** — receive your data in a portable format
- **Right to object** — object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Right to withdraw consent** — at any time for consent-based processing

### POPIA (South African users)
- Right to access your personal information
- Right to correction or deletion
- Right to object to processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

### How to Exercise Your Rights
Submit requests to: **privacy@metups.com**  
Response time: Within **30 days** of verified request

## 8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures including:
- TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
- JWT-based session tokens with expiry
- Input validation and parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection
- Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
- Regular security reviews

See our [Security Policy](../security/security-policy.md) for full details.
