## 1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to any fees paid directly to Metups (e.g., future premium listing fees or promotional boosts). It does **not** govern transactions between buyers and sellers — those are private arrangements.

---

## 2. Current Fee Structure

Metups is currently **free to use**. No payment is required to list, browse, or contact sellers. This section will be updated when paid features are introduced.

---

## 3. Peer-to-Peer Transactions

Metups does not process payments between buyers and sellers. All transactions occur privately between users. Metups has no liability for:
- Items not as described
- Failed meetups
- Payment disputes
- Fraud between users

**Buyers are strongly advised to inspect items before payment.**

---

## 4. Platform Fees (Future Premium Features)

When premium features are introduced, the following refund terms will apply:

### 4.1 Listing Promotions / Boosts
- Refundable within **24 hours** of purchase if the listing has not yet been boosted
- Non-refundable once the promotional period has started

### 4.2 Subscription Plans (if applicable)
- See [Subscription Policy](subscription-policy.md)
- Monthly subscriptions: pro-rated refund within 7 days of initial purchase
- Annual subscriptions: pro-rated refund within 14 days of initial purchase

### 4.3 Technical Errors
- Full refund issued if a technical error on our part resulted in failed delivery of a paid service
- Refund processed within 5–10 business days to original payment method

---

## 5. How to Request a Refund

1. Email **billing@metups.com** with subject line: `REFUND REQUEST - [ORDER ID]`
2. Include your account email, order ID, and reason for refund
3. We will acknowledge within **2 business days**
4. Decision communicated within **5 business days**
