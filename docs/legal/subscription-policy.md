## 3. Billing Terms

3.1 Subscriptions are billed in advance on a recurring cycle.  
3.2 Payment is processed via [PAYMENT PROVIDER — e.g., Stripe, PayFast, Paynow Zimbabwe].  
3.3 Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled before the renewal date.  
3.4 You will receive a reminder email 7 days before renewal.  

---

## 4. Cancellation

4.1 Cancel at any time via Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan.  
4.2 Access continues until the end of the current billing period.  
4.3 No partial refunds for unused time unless covered by the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).  

---

## 5. Downgrades and Upgrades

5.1 **Upgrades** take effect immediately; you are charged the pro-rated difference.  
5.2 **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period.  
5.3 Listings above the free tier limit will be deactivated (not deleted) upon downgrade.  

---

## 6. Failed Payments

6.1 If a payment fails, we will retry 3 times over 7 days.  
6.2 If still unpaid, the subscription will be downgraded to Free.  
6.3 Listings are preserved for 30 days and reactivated if payment is resolved.
