## Welcome to Metups

Metups is Zimbabwe's free marketplace for buying and selling pre-owned goods. This manual walks you through every feature of the platform.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit **metups.com** on your phone or computer
2. Tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your **email address** and create a **password** (minimum 8 characters)
4. Tap **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for a **verification link** and click it
6. You're in!

**Alternatively:** Tap **"Continue with Google"** to sign in instantly using your Google account — no password needed.

---

### Installing Metups as an App (PWA)

Install Metups directly to your phone's home screen — no app store needed:

**Android (Chrome):**
1. Open metups.com in Chrome
2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) → **"Add to Home screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**

**iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open metups.com in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
3. Scroll down → **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**

## Browsing Listings

### The Home Feed
The home screen shows the latest listings near you. Scroll down to load more.

### Searching
Tap the **search bar** at the top and type what you're looking for (e.g., "iPhone", "sofa", "bicycle").

### Filtering
Use filters to narrow results:
- **Category** — Electronics, Furniture, Clothing, Vehicles, etc.
- **Location** — Browse by city
- **Price range** — Set minimum and maximum price

### Viewing a Listing
Tap any listing card to see:
- Full description
- All photos (swipe or tap to browse)
- Seller profile and rating
- Location (city)
- Price

---

## Contacting a Seller

1. Open a listing you're interested in
2. Tap **"Message Seller"**
3. Type your message and tap **Send**
4. The seller will receive a notification and reply in the chat

**Tips:**
- Introduce yourself and confirm the item is still available
- Arrange to view the item before paying
- Agree on a safe public meeting place

---

## Saving Items (Wishlist)

Tap the **heart icon ♥** on any listing to save it to your Wishlist.  
Access your Wishlist from the bottom navigation bar.

---

## Selling an Item

### Creating a Listing

1. Tap the **"+"** button in the bottom navigation
2. Add photos (tap the camera icon) — at least 3 photos recommended
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — be specific (e.g., "iPhone 12 64GB Black Good Condition")
   - **Description** — include condition, any defects, reason for selling
   - **Price** — in USD
   - **Category** — select the best match
   - **Condition** — Like New / Good / Fair / For Parts
   - **Location** — your city/area
4. Tap **"Publish Listing"**

**Tips for great listings:**
- Photos in good lighting from multiple angles sell faster
- Be honest about condition — build your reputation
- Price fairly — check what similar items sell for

### Managing Your Listings
Go to **Profile → My Listings** to:
- Edit a listing
- Delete a listing
- Mark as sold

### Marking an Item as Sold
Once you've completed a transaction:
1. Go to My Listings
2. Tap the listing
3. Tap **"Mark as Sold"**

This removes it from the public feed but keeps it in your history.

## Messages

Access all your conversations from the **Chat** icon in the bottom navigation.

Tap a conversation to:
- Continue the chat
- View the listing you're discussing
- See the seller/buyer's profile

## Notifications

Metups will notify you when:
- A buyer messages you about a listing
- A seller replies to your message
- A listing you saved drops in price (future feature)

Manage notifications at: **Profile → Settings → Notifications**

## Your Profile

### Editing Your Profile
Go to **Profile → Edit Profile** to:
- Update your display name
- Change your profile photo
- Update your city
- Add or verify your phone number

### Your Rating
After a transaction, buyers and sellers can rate each other (1–5 stars). Your average rating is displayed on your profile and builds trust with the community.

## Safety Tips

1. **Meet in public** — shopping centres, police stations, busy areas
2. **Bring a friend** — especially for high-value items
3. **Inspect before paying** — test electronics, check condition
4. **Never send money in advance** — Metups does not process payments; always meet in person
5. **Trust your instincts** — if something feels wrong, walk away
6. **Report suspicious behaviour** — tap "Report" on any listing or message
