## Our Commitment

Metups is committed to the security of our users' data and the platform. We value the security research community and welcome responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities. We will work with you to verify and remediate issues and will acknowledge your contribution.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Email:** security@metups.com  
**PGP Key:** [PUBLISH PGP KEY OR LINK]  
**Response:** We will acknowledge receipt within **48 hours** and provide a status update within **7 days**.

### What to Include in Your Report
- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce (proof of concept)
- Potential impact assessment
- Any supporting screenshots or logs
- Your contact details (optional but appreciated for acknowledgement)

---

## In-Scope Systems

The following are in scope for vulnerability research:

- metups.com and all subdomains
- The Metups Progressive Web App
- Supabase API endpoints used by Metups (within Metups' control)

---

## Out of Scope

The following are **not** in scope:

- Vulnerabilities in Supabase, Cloudflare, or Google infrastructure (report these directly to the vendor)
- Social engineering attacks on Metups staff
- Physical attacks
- Denial of service attacks
- Automated scanning tools that generate excessive load
- Issues requiring physical access to a victim's device
- Vulnerabilities in third-party libraries (report to library maintainer)
- Rate limiting issues without demonstrated exploitability

---

## Rules of Engagement

When researching vulnerabilities, you must:
- **Not** access, modify, or delete user data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability
- **Not** perform any action that could harm other users
- **Not** conduct denial-of-service testing
- **Not** use findings for personal gain or share with third parties before disclosure
- **Not** violate Zimbabwean or applicable international law

---

## Our Commitments to Researchers

- Acknowledge your report within 48 hours
- Provide a status update within 7 days
- Not pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith under this policy
- Work to remediate critical/high vulnerabilities within 30 days
- Credit researchers (by name or alias) in our Security Hall of Fame upon their request
- [Bug bounty programme is under consideration — to be announced]
