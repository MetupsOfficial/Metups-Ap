About Metups

Metups is a Zimbabwe-focused marketplace for finding and offering pre-owned goods. The service brings listings together in one place so people can discover items, see the details provided by the seller and start a conversation about a possible purchase.

A local marketplace

People often have useful goods they no longer need, while someone nearby may be looking for exactly those things. Metups is built around that simple exchange. Buyers can browse categories including electronics, fashion, furniture, appliances, vehicles and books, and use location information to explore what is available in their area. Listings include seller-provided details such as title, description, price, condition, location and images.

Tools for buyers and sellers

Buyers can search listings, review item information, save items to a wishlist and contact sellers through the platform. Sellers use the Metups website or app to create, edit and manage their listings. WhatsApp is a buyer support and discovery channel; listing creation takes place on the Metups service. The contact and negotiation happen between users, who can ask questions and decide whether the item and terms suit them.

Clear expectations

Metups provides marketplace technology and rules for using it. A listing is not a guarantee that an item is available, authentic, safe or in the condition expected. Sellers are responsible for accurate listings, and buyers should review details, ask questions and inspect an item before completing a transaction. Users arrange payment and collection directly. Read the terms of service and acceptable use policy for the requirements that apply to listings and user conduct.

Safety and support

We encourage users to protect their personal information, choose sensible handover arrangements and report suspicious activity. Our buyer safety guide and seller onboarding guide offer practical steps for using the marketplace. If you need help with an account, listing or safety concern, visit Contact Metups. You can also learn how the marketplace works or support the service.
