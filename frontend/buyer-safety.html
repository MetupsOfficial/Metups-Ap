Buyer safety guide

Metups helps buyers find pre-owned goods listed by sellers. A listing and a chat can help you assess an item, but they do not replace your own checks. Take time to confirm the details and agree on a safe handover before paying.

Review the listing carefully

Read the complete title, description, condition and location. Look closely at every photo and ask the seller for recent images of any area that matters to you. For electronics, ask about functionality, battery health, accessories, repairs and known faults. For furniture, vehicles and appliances, confirm dimensions or other specifications that affect whether the item will work for you. If details are missing or contradictory, ask for clarification in the Metups conversation. Do not assume that a photo or short description proves ownership, authenticity or working condition.

Ask direct questions

Before travelling, confirm that the item is still available, the agreed price, the collection location and any delivery arrangement. Keep important terms in the conversation so both sides can refer to them. Be cautious if a seller pressures you to act immediately, changes the arrangement repeatedly, refuses reasonable questions, or asks you to move to an unusual payment or communication method. Never share your Metups password, email access code, one-time pin or banking credentials with another user.

Plan a safe inspection and handover

When possible, meet during daylight in a busy public place and tell someone you trust where you are going. Consider bringing another person. Inspect the item before paying and test it where practical. For a high-value purchase, arrange a suitable location and enough time to check the item. Do not send money in advance to reserve an item unless you understand and accept the risk. Metups does not hold payment or guarantee transactions between buyers and sellers; agree payment and collection directly and keep a record of what you agreed.

Protect your information

Share only the information needed to complete the exchange. Avoid posting sensitive personal details in listing comments or public descriptions. Do not disclose identity documents or account credentials to someone who has no clear need for them. Review the privacy policy to understand how Metups handles account and listing information.

Report concerns

If a listing appears deceptive, prohibited or suspicious, use the report option where available and contact trust@metups.com. If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Read the acceptable use policy and terms for the marketplace rules. For general questions, visit Contact Metups.

These suggestions are general precautions and cannot eliminate risk. Use your judgement throughout the transaction.
