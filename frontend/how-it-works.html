How Metups works

Metups helps people in Zimbabwe discover pre-owned goods offered by other people in their communities. Buyers can browse listings, compare prices and item conditions, and contact a seller through the listing. Sellers create and manage listings through the Metups app or website.

Find something you need

Start by browsing the latest listings or use the search and filters to narrow the selection. You can look through categories such as electronics, fashion, furniture, appliances, vehicles and books. Location details help you find items offered near you. Each listing should describe the item, show its price and condition, and include photos where available. Read the full description and inspect the photos before deciding whether to contact the seller.

Talk with the seller

Open a listing to see its details. If you want to know more, use the contact option on the page to start a conversation with the seller. Ask clear questions about the item's condition, included accessories, age, defects, collection arrangements and whether it is still available. Agree on a price and a safe handover directly with the seller. Metups provides the listing and communication tools; buyers and sellers make their own arrangements for payment and collection.

List an item

To sell, create an account and use the listing form on Metups. Choose a suitable category, write an accurate title and description, set a price, select the item's condition and location, then add clear photos. Review all details before publishing. Keep your listing up to date and mark an item as sold when it is no longer available. Do not post misleading descriptions, stolen property, prohibited items or images that do not represent the item.

Use the marketplace thoughtfully

Metups is designed to make local discovery straightforward. There is no listing fee described in the marketplace offer, but users should review current site information and terms. Meet in a public place when practical, check the item before paying, and do not share passwords or verification codes. Report suspicious listings or conduct through the listing tools or the contact details on our contact page. Read our buyer safety guide, seller guide and terms before using the service.
