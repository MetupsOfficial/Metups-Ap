Acceptable Use

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026  ·  Version 1.0

This policy defines what is and isn't allowed on Metups. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or legal action.

What You Can Do

  • Post honest listings of pre-owned items you own or have authority to sell
  • Browse, save, and enquire about listings
  • Communicate with other users for legitimate transaction purposes
  • Rate and review transaction counterparties honestly

Prohibited Items

  • Drugs, controlled substances, or paraphernalia
  • Firearms, weapons, or ammunition without appropriate licence
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods
  • Items that infringe copyright, trademark, or patent
  • Wildlife or protected species
  • Recalled or safety-hazard products
  • Adult content or services
  • Any item illegal under Zimbabwean law

Prohibited Behaviour

Fraud & Deception

  • Misrepresenting items in photos, descriptions, or condition
  • Advance-fee scams or fake escrow requests
  • Listings for items you do not possess

Harassment & Harm

  • Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any user
  • Sharing other users' private information ("doxxing")
  • Impersonating Metups staff, officials, or other users

Platform Abuse

  • Creating multiple accounts to evade suspension
  • Automated bulk listing creation without approval
  • Scraping or harvesting user data
  • Manipulating ratings through fake transactions

Enforcement

SeverityAction
Minor first offenceWarning + content removal
Repeated minor violations7-day suspension
Serious violationPermanent ban
Illegal activityPermanent ban + report to authorities

Reporting Violations

Tap Report on any listing, message, or user profile. Or email trust@metups.com. We review reports within 24–48 hours.

Appeals

Appeal a moderation decision within 14 days by emailing appeals@metups.com.
