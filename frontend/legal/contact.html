Quick Answers

How do I reset my password?
On the login page, tap "Forgot Password", enter your email, and follow the link sent to your inbox.
How do I report a scam or suspicious listing?
Tap the Report button on any listing or user profile, or email trust@metups.com.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
The app won't load — what do I do?
Check your internet connection, then refresh the page. If using the installed PWA, try opening metups.com directly in Chrome.
