What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Metups also uses localStorage and sessionStorage for similar purposes. This policy covers all such technologies.

PWA & Offline Storage

As a Progressive Web App, Metups caches static assets (HTML, CSS, JS, images) locally on your device so the app works faster and partially offline. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. Clear it via your browser settings or by uninstalling the app.

No Advertising Cookies

We do not use advertising, tracking, or cross-site cookies of any kind.

Managing Cookies

  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies
  • App settings: Metups → Settings → Privacy for functional cookie preferences

Disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in.
