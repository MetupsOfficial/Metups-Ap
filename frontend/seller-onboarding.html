Seller onboarding guide

Metups gives sellers a way to present pre-owned goods to buyers in Zimbabwe. A useful listing answers common questions before a buyer contacts you. Create and manage listings through the Metups website or app; WhatsApp does not create listings.

Prepare your item

Make sure the item is yours to sell and allowed under the acceptable use policy. Clean it where appropriate and check its condition. Note defects, missing parts, repairs or limitations that a buyer should know. For electronics and appliances, test the functions you can and describe any issue plainly. Do not claim an item is new, original or fully working unless that is accurate. Clear, honest information helps buyers decide whether to contact you and can prevent misunderstandings later.

Create a complete listing

Choose the category that best fits the item. Write a specific title that identifies the product and use a description to add relevant details such as model, size, age, included accessories and known wear. Select the condition that reflects the actual item, enter a fair price and choose the correct location. Add clear photos of the item itself, taken in good light. Include important details in the photos and avoid images that hide damage or show a different product. Do not place phone numbers, email addresses, passwords or other sensitive personal data in public listing text.

Respond and arrange the exchange

Check your Metups messages and answer questions accurately. Confirm availability, price, collection or delivery arrangements before a buyer travels. Agree payment and handover details directly with the buyer. Do not share account passwords or verification codes, even if someone claims they need them to arrange a sale. Meet in a sensible place, and consider telling someone you trust about the arrangement. Keep a record of agreed details in the conversation.

Keep the listing current

Update the price, description or photos when circumstances change. If the item is no longer available, mark it as sold or remove the listing using the account tools. Prompt updates help buyers avoid contacting you about an unavailable item. Use the report and support channels if you encounter suspicious behaviour or need account assistance.

Follow the marketplace rules

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of your listing and for complying with the terms of service. Do not list stolen goods, prohibited items, counterfeit goods, or anything you cannot lawfully sell. Do not misrepresent the product, harass buyers or attempt to obtain their credentials. Review the buyer safety guide, and contact Metups through the contact page when you need help. Metups provides marketplace tools; sellers and buyers independently agree on any sale and its fulfilment.
