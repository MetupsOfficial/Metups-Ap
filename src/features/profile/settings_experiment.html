Data & Privacy Policy
How we handle your data

What we collect

Your name, email, location, and listings. Messages are stored to deliver them to recipients. We never sell your data.

How we use it

To operate the marketplace, show your listings to buyers, send notifications you've enabled, and improve the app.

Your rights

You can edit or delete your listings at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact support@metups.com.

Storage

Data is stored on Supabase servers. Profile images and product photos are stored in secure cloud storage. Messages are encrypted in transit.
