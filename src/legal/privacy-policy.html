Information you provide

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, browser version, operating system

From third parties

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data (via Settings → Profile)
  • Delete your account and data (via Settings → Delete Account)
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to marketing (unsubscribe any time)

To exercise these rights, email privacy@metups.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, Supabase Row Level Security (so users can only see their own private data), JWT authentication tokens, and regular security reviews. Passwords are hashed — we cannot read them.

8. Children

Metups is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact privacy@metups.com if you believe a child has registered.

9. Changes to This Policy
